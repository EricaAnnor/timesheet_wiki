Using the Timesheet Bot

A Teams bot that builds and keeps your monthly timesheet up to date automatically — no spreadsheets to open, no forms to fill.

What it does

It runs two things for you automatically, working together on the same file:

1st of every month

Creates your timesheet

A fresh, correctly formatted timesheet for the new month is generated and placed in your OneDrive automatically. You never have to create one yourself.

Every evening, ~5pm

Logs your day

The bot asks what you achieved in Teams. You reply, and it writes the entry straight into that month's timesheet — correct day, correct row.

Getting started

  1. Open Microsoft Teams Desktop app, mobile app, or teams.microsoft.com — any of them work.
  2. Go to Apps, search "Timesheet Bot" It's already published for eBASE Africa, so it'll show up directly — no approval or install request needed.
    Open it and add it to your chats like you would any other app.
  3. Send it one message — anything, even just "hi"
    This is the step that actually matters: it's what lets the bot find you later. Skip it, and you won't receive a daily prompt.

What happens every day

Around 5:00pm your local time, the bot sends you a message asking what you achieved. Reply in the same chat.

Timesheet BotWhat did you achieve today? Reply with one activity per line, formatted as: activity - location - hours

The format

One activity per line. Three parts, separated by a dash:

activity - location - hours

Several activities in one day? Send them as separate lines in the same reply — the bot handles all of them at once. Working on the same task across several days this week? Just log it each day; the bot recognizes it's the same activity and adds the hours to the right day automatically, rather than creating duplicate entries.

Missed a day?

If you don't reply one evening, the next time you do reply, the bot attributes it to the day it actually asked about — not the day you happened to answer. You don't need to do anything differently; just reply when you're ready, and you'll see a note confirming which day it logged.

Finding your timesheet

Your monthly timesheet lives in your own OneDrive, generated automatically on the 1st of each month:

OneDrive → Timesheets folder → <Your Name> - <Month Year> Timesheet.xlsx

Tip: if a search for the file comes up empty, navigate to the Timesheets folder directly instead — search can lag a little behind a freshly created file.
Important: if you have the file open (in Excel, or a browser tab) when the bot tries to log your entry, it can't save — close it before replying to the daily prompt, or right after if you had it open for another reason.

Questions

What if I make a typo or send something the bot doesn't understand?

It tells you exactly which line it couldn't read and why, and still logs whichever lines were valid — one bad line never blocks the rest of your message.

I logged something wrong. Can I fix it?

Yes — it's your file. Open it from OneDrive and correct the activity, location, or hours directly in the cell, the same as you'd fix a typo in any spreadsheet. Just edit the values in place rather than deleting or adding rows yourself; the bot manages rows and totals automatically, and a manual insert or delete can throw those off. If you do run into that, reach out to the iCODE team.

The bot said it couldn't save my entry — what does that mean?

It means your timesheet file was open somewhere (Excel, a browser tab) at the exact moment it tried to write to it — OneDrive locks a file while it's open, the same way it would for anyone else trying to edit it at the same time. Close the file and resend your message; nothing was lost.

Does it work on weekends?

Yes — the prompt goes out every day, including weekends, in case you worked. No reply, no entry — it never assumes.

The bot didn't message me today.

Double-check you completed step 3 above (sending it a first message). If you did and still didn't hear from it, contact the iCODE team.
